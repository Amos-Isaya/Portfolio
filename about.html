About

Hi, I’m Isaya. I’m a Computer Information Systems student at Livingstone College, and I enjoy learning how technology works by building with it.

I came to the United States from Rwanda to continue my education. The journey taught me how to adjust to change, ask questions, and keep working when the next step is not obvious. NextGen4All helped me with college applications and interviews, and that support helped lead me to Livingstone College.

I’m interested in software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and cybersecurity. I have built tools for student productivity and campus safety, and I have used data and security ideas in a team hackathon project.

I’m still early in my career, so I do not pretend to know everything. What I bring is curiosity, discipline, teamwork, and the willingness to learn from feedback.

What I’m good at: Learning quickly, working with a team, explaining ideas, and staying focused on the problem
